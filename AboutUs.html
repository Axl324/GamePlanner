About Us

We are a team of students from La Salle URL who love video games and know the problem well: a backlog that keeps growing while our free time does not.

Our mission

Help players organize the games they own, keep track of the time they spend on each one and spend less time deciding what to play and more time playing.

Our vision

We want GamePlanner+ to be a simple, free and private place where any player can turn a forgotten list of games into a clear plan. Your data stays in your browser, and no account is needed.
