Welcome to GamePlanner+, the place where your games stop piling up. Sort your titles into collections, log every gaming session and get suggestions for your next game, all from your browser.

Everything you need to manage your games

GamePlanner+ brings together the tools that a player uses every day.

Collection management

Create collections such as "Currently Playing", "Pending" o "Completed", give each one a name, a category and a description, and edit or delete them whenever you want.

Gaming session log

Record the date, the hours played and your notes for every session, so you always know how far you got in each game.

Game Explorer

Search more than 500,000 games by name, genre or platform. See the cover, rating and genre of each title and add it to a collection in one step.

GameGuide assistant

Not sure what to play tonight? Tell GameGuide your favourite genres or how much time you have, and it will suggest your next game.

How it works

  1. Create a collection

    Start with a name, a category and a short description.

  2. Add your games

    Find any title in the Game Explorer and add it to one of your collections.

  3. Log your sessions

    Write down the date, the hours played and your progress after each session.

  4. Pick your next game

    Ask GameGuide for a suggestion based on your genres and free time.

Meet GamePlanner+

Most players have more games than time. GamePlanner+ was created to turn a long, forgotten list of games into a clear plan: what you are playing now, what is waiting and what you have already finished.

Your data is saved in your own browser, so you can close the page and find your collections exactly where you left them. No account is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is GamePlanner+ really free?

Yes. Every feature is included in the Free Forever plan and there are no hidden payments.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start creating collections as soon as you open the page.

Where are my collections saved?

They are saved in your browser's local storage. Your data stays on your device and is still there after you close the browser.

Where does the game information come from?

Names, genres, ratings and covers come from the RAWG Video Games Database, a public database with more than 500,000 games.

What can GameGuide do?

GameGuide is a chat assistant powered by Google's Gemini. It gives you ideas for your next game based on your favourite genres, your available time or your preferences.
